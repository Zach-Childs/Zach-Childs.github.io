About Imitari

Imitari begins with a belief: every person carries a dignity that deserves to be seen, honored and returned to them. What we make is meant to give it back.

We pursue perfection in all things. We hold the stitch, the finish and the weight of every piece to that standard, because the details are where respect shows.

Imitari is Latin for “to imitate.” We chose the word on purpose. Work this carefully made gets noticed, and what gets noticed gets copied.

Be envied.Be inspiring.Be imitated.
